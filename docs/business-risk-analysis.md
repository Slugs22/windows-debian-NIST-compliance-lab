\# Business Risk Analysis



\## Purpose

This document analyzes the business risk associated with Open findings and exceptions documented in this lab. This mirrors the risk acceptance process used by Authorizing Officials (AOs) in real government and enterprise environments under NIST RMF and FISMA.



\## Risk Rating Definitions

* \*\*CAT I / High:\*\* Direct and immediate threat. Exploitation could result in loss of confidentiality, integrity, or availability.\\
* \*\*CAT II / Medium:\*\* Significant risk but requires additional conditions to exploit.
* \*\*CAT III / Low:\*\* Minimal risk. Minimal impact if exploited.

\## Risk Acceptance Process

For each Open finding the following is documented:

1. Finding ID and title
2. Severity rating
3. Reason it cannot be remediated
4. Compensating controls in place
5. Residual risk rating after compensating controls
6. Recommended fix when resources become available



\## Regulatory Context

* Federal agencies follow FISMA and NIST SP 800-53
* DoD systems follow DoDI 85000.01 and DODI 8510.01
* Control CM-6 requires secure configuration baselines
* Deviations require AO approval and POA\&M tracking
* This lab stimulates that process in a non-production environment
